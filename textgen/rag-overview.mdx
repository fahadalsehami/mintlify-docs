Medera's Clinical RAG system runs hybrid semantic + keyword search over medical ontologies, clinical guidelines, drug interactions, and payer-specific authorization rules.

## Layers

- **Semantic retrieval:** ChromaDB vector index over the clinical knowledge base
- **Keyword retrieval:** BM25 over the same corpus
- **Ontology expansion:** SNOMED CT, ICD-10, RxNorm, LOINC term expansion
- **Query rewriting:** clinical context-aware reformulation
- **Citation tracking:** every retrieved chunk carries provenance

## Confidence calibration

Retrieval confidence: `VERY_HIGH` → `HIGH` → `MODERATE` → `LOW` → `VERY_LOW`. Below `MODERATE`, results are surfaced with `requires_human_review: true`.
