---
title: "Introduction to Templates"
description: "Working with Medera templates for document generation"
---


Templates are versioned, schema-validated definitions of a document's sections and required content. Medera ships a library of standard templates (SOAP, referral, discharge, etc.) and supports custom templates per practice.

## Template lifecycle

```
draft → review → published → deprecated → archived
```

Only `published` templates can drive document generation in production.

## Versioning

Templates are semver-versioned. Breaking changes (renamed section keys, removed required fields) bump major; non-breaking changes bump minor.
