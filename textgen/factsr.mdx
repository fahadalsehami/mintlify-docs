---
title: "Introducing Facts & Reasoning"
description: "Real-time clinical reasoning over the encounter"
---


Medera's Facts & Reasoning system extracts a structured `Facts` object from the encounter — every clinically relevant claim, who said it, when, and with what certainty. Documents generate against the fact set, not against raw transcripts.

## Why facts?

- **Defensibility:** every claim in a generated document ties back to a fact
- **Editability:** the clinician can correct a fact, and downstream documents update
- **Audit:** facts are an auditable, immutable layer between transcript and note

## Fact schema

```json
{
  "fact_id": "fact_abc",
  "group": "presenting_problem",
  "claim": "Persistent low mood for 6 weeks",
  "evidence_spans": ["span_004"],
  "source_speaker": "patient",
  "certainty": "asserted",
  "timestamp_ms": 84300
}
```
