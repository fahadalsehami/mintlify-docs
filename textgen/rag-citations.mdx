---
title: "Citations"
description: "Citation provenance and rendering"
---


Every retrieved chunk carries provenance:

```json
{
  "chunk_id": "ch_abc",
  "source": "APA",
  "document": "APA Clinical Practice Guideline for MDD",
  "section": "Pharmacotherapy",
  "url": "https://...",
  "publication_date": "2024-01-15",
  "currency": "current"
}
```

Render citations in the surface that consumes the retrieval — every clinical claim in a generated document should anchor to a citation or a transcript span.
