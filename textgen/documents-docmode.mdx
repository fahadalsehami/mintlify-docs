---
title: "Documentation Modes"
description: "Differences between documentation modes"
---


| Mode | Use case | Latency | Recommended |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `realtime` | In-encounter drafting | Sub-second per section | Visit Scribe |
| `batch` | Post-visit assembly | Seconds | Async ambient |
| `regen` | Re-generate from facts | Seconds | Template change, late edits |
