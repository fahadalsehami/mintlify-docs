---
title: "Data Residency"
description: "US, EU, and customer-VPC options"
---


| Region | Storage | Compute | Sub-processors |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| US (default) | US-East, US-West | US | US-only |
| EU | EU-West (Ireland), EU-Central (Frankfurt) | EU | EU-only |
| Customer VPC | Customer-managed AWS / GCP VPC | Customer | Per customer agreement |

Region is selected at tenant creation and is immutable. PHI never crosses region boundaries.
