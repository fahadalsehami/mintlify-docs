---
title: "MFA / SSO"
description: "Multi-factor authentication and SSO"
--- Medera supports: - **MFA** via TOTP, SMS, and WebAuthn (Passkeys)
- **SSO** via SAML 2.0 and OIDC
- **Just-in-time provisioning** for SSO users
- **SCIM** for user lifecycle management Enterprise tenants require MFA on all administrative users.
