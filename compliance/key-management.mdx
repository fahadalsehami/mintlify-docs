---
title: "Key Management"
description: "Per-tenant data encryption keys and KEK rotation"
---


Each tenant has a unique **Data Encryption Key (DEK)** for PHI columns. DEKs are wrapped by a **Key Encryption Key (KEK)** stored in AWS KMS (or customer-managed CloudHSM).

## Rotation

- DEK rotation: quarterly, transparent re-encryption
- KEK rotation: annual

Customers can opt into customer-managed keys (CMK) for the KEK layer.
