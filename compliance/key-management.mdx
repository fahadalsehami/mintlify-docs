---
title: "Key Management"
description: "Per-tenant data encryption keys and KEK rotation"
--- Each tenant has a unique **Data Encryption Key (DEK)** for PHI columns. DEKs are wrapped by a **Key Encryption Key (KEK)** stored in managed KMS (or customer-managed hardware security module). ## Rotation - DEK rotation: quarterly, transparent re-encryption
- KEK rotation: annual Customers can opt into customer-managed keys (CMK) for the KEK layer.
