Sub-processors handle specific functions on Medera's behalf. The list below is current as of platform release; material changes trigger a **30-day notice** to security and compliance contacts on file.

## US tenants

| Sub-processor | Purpose | Region |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| AWS | Compute, storage, KMS | US region |
| Medera reasoning | LLM inference (Medera-managed) | US |
| voice carrier | Voice / SMS for intake agents | US |
| Medera Voice | Voice orchestration | US |
| Medera STT | Medera STT transcription (Co-Therapy pipeline) | US |
| Medera voice | Voice synthesis (Intake, Care Coordinator, Referral Specialist, PA Specialist) | US |
| observability platform | Observability | US |
| error tracking | Error tracking | US |
| payment processor | Billing | US |
| the identity provider | Authentication | US |

## EU tenants

| Sub-processor | Purpose | Region |

## MENA /

Customer VPC Sub-processors per customer agreement.

Typical setup keeps sub-processors region-local; LLM inference may use a customer-hosted endpoint.

## Change notification

Material changes (new sub-processor, region change, processing change) trigger:

- 30-day advance notice to your tenant security contact

- Update to this page

- Optional Trust Center notification preference Contact [hi@medera.info](mailto:hi@medera.info) to subscribe to change notifications.
