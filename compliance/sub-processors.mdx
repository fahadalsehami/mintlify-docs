Sub-processors handle specific functions on Medera's behalf. The list below is current as of platform release; material changes trigger a **30-day notice** to security and compliance contacts on file.

## US tenants

| Sub-processor | Purpose | Region |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| AWS | Compute, storage, KMS | US-East, US-West |
| Anthropic / OpenAI | LLM inference (Medera-managed) | US |
| Twilio | Voice / SMS for intake agents | US |
| Vapi | Voice orchestration | US |
| Deepgram | nova-2-medical transcription (Co-Therapy pipeline) | US |
| ElevenLabs | Voice synthesis (Grace, Alex, Jordan, Taylor) | US |
| Datadog | Observability | US |
| Sentry | Error tracking | US |
| Stripe | Billing | US |
| Clerk | Authentication | US |

## MENA / Customer VPC

Sub-processors per customer agreement. Typical setup keeps sub-processors region-local; LLM inference may use a customer-hosted endpoint.

## Change notification

Material changes (new sub-processor, region change, processing change) trigger:
- 30-day advance notice to your tenant security contact
- Update to this page
- Optional Trust Center notification preference

Contact [compliance@medera.info](mailto:compliance@medera.info) to subscribe to change notifications.
