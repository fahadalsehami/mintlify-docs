Medera's Multimodal Sensing layer fuses three real-time clinical signal channels — speech, facial physiology, and vocal acoustics — into a single defensible read of the patient's state.

## The three engines

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Speech to Text" icon="audio-lines" href="/multimodal/stt/overview">
    Whisper + Deepgram nova-2-medical with sub-second interim results, diarization, and dictation commands.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Facial Physiology" icon="eye" href="/multimodal/facial/overview">
    HR, BP, HRV (SDNN/RMSSD/LF-HF), respiration, stress index, affect from MediaPipe 468-point landmarks.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Vocal Acoustics" icon="waveform-lines" href="/multimodal/vocal/overview">
    F0, jitter, shimmer, HNR, MFCC, prosodic flatness, depression/anxiety/distress indices.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

The three engines feed the [RDoC Construct Computer](/multimodal/rdoc/overview), which produces 15 documented construct activations with feature contributions and confidence.

## Architecture at a glance

```
   AUDIO  →  Whisper · Deepgram nova-2-medical  →  Diarized transcript
            ↓
            Vocal Acoustic Engine (Librosa + Parselmouth)
            ↓
            ┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
   VIDEO →  │   RDoC Construct Computer (15 RDoC)     │
            │   ↑ Vocal · Facial · Linguistic         │
   LANG  →  └─────────────────────────────────────────┘
            ↓
            Construct Activations + Clinical Markers
```

## Quality and confidence

Every multimodal output reports a confidence band (`VERY_HIGH` → `VERY_LOW`) and the feature contributions that drove it. Clinical claims are bounded to the documented signal — never extrapolated.

## What you can build

- Real-time SOAP scribe with multimodal anchors
- Co-Therapy in-session signal channel for the clinician
- Adaptive intake with prosody-driven branching
- Longitudinal RDoC tracking across episodes
