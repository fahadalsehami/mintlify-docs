The Multimodal Sensing layer is built around three independent engines plus a fusion layer.

## Engines

### Audio → Vocal Acoustic Engine
- **Input:** 16 kHz mono PCM
- **Tools:** Librosa, Parselmouth (Praat), TorchAudio
- **Outputs:** F0 (mean, std, min, max, range), jitter (%), shimmer (%), HNR (dB), MFCCs, spectral centroid/bandwidth/rolloff/flatness, speaking rate (WPM), articulation rate, pause patterns
- **Clinical markers:** depression index, anxiety index, distress index, prosodic flatness
- **Requirements:** 16 kHz, min 10s recording, 25 ms frame / 10 ms hop

### Video → Facial Physiological Engine
- **Input:** Video frames (≥ 15 fps recommended)
- **Tools:** MediaPipe 468-point landmarks, OpenCV
- **Outputs:**
  - Heart Rate (HR) — <5 bpm MAE, min 30 s recording
  - Blood Pressure (systolic / diastolic) with confidence
  - HRV — SDNN, RMSSD, LF/HF, SD1, SD2 (min 60 s)
  - Respiration Rate (RR)
  - Stress Index (0–100) with ANS balance (PNS/SNS)
  - Emotion detection (6 basic + neutral)
  - Microexpression tracking + engagement scoring
- **Signal quality:** SNR > 3.0 required

### Language → Linguistic Content Expert
- **Input:** Diarized transcript
- **Outputs:** topic, valence, certainty, pronoun-shift markers, validated screener candidates

## Fusion → RDoC Construct Computer

The fusion layer maps multimodal features to 15 RDoC constructs across 5 domains with zero overlap. See [RDoC Overview](/multimodal/rdoc/overview).
