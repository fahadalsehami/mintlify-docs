---
title: "Heart Rate"
description: "Continuous HR from facial video"
---


Heart rate is computed from rPPG signals extracted from forehead and cheek ROIs.

- **Accuracy:** <5 bpm MAE against pulse oximeter ground truth
- **Min duration:** 30 s
- **Window:** Sliding 10 s, 1 s hop
- **Output:** Beats per minute, confidence
