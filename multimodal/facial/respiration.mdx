---
title: "Respiration Rate"
description: "Breaths per minute from rPPG amplitude modulation"
---

RR is computed by `_extract_respiration_rate(rgb_traces, fps)` from rPPG amplitude envelope.

| Field | Unit |
| :--- | :--- |
| `respiration_rate` | breaths / minute |
| `respiration_confidence` | 0–1 |

Minimum duration: 30 s.
