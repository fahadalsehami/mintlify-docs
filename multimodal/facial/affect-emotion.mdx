description: "Emotion detection + microexpressions + engagement"
---


Per-frame affect classification across 6 basic emotions + neutral, plus microexpression onset/offset tracking and an engagement score.

- **Emotion classes:** anger, disgust, fear, happiness, sadness, surprise, neutral
- **Microexpression window:** 40–200 ms
- **Engagement:** 0–1 composite
