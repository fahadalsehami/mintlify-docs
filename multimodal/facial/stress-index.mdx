---
title: "Stress Index"
description: "0–100 stress score with ANS balance"
---


The Stress Index fuses HRV, RR, and affect into a 0–100 stress score and reports the inferred autonomic balance (PNS vs SNS dominance).

- **Output:** Stress (0–100), PNS contribution, SNS contribution, confidence
- **Min duration:** 60 s
