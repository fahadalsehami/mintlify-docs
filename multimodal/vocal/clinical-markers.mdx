---


The Vocal Acoustic Engine fuses raw features into clinical markers calibrated for behavioral health.

| Marker | Composite |
| :--- | :--- |
| Depression Index | F0 range ↓, speaking rate ↓, jitter ↑ |
| Anxiety Index | F0 mean ↑, shimmer irregularity ↑ |
| Distress Index | Jitter ↑, shimmer ↑, HNR ↓ |
| Prosodic Flatness | F0 std ↓ |

Every marker reports a confidence and the feature contributions that drove it. The markers are not a diagnosis — they are signal for the clinician.
