---
title: "Voice Quality (Jitter, Shimmer, HNR)"
description: "Cycle-to-cycle voice quality measures"
---


| Feature | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| Jitter (%) | Cycle-to-cycle F0 variation |
| Shimmer (%) | Cycle-to-cycle amplitude variation |
| HNR (dB) | Harmonics-to-noise ratio |

Elevated jitter and shimmer correlate with vocal effort, fatigue, and emotional distress. Decreased HNR signals breathiness or hoarseness.
