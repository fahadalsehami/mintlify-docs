---
title: "Formatting"
description: "How dictated text is formatted for output"
---

Default formatting rules:

- Numbers spoken as digits when amounts (`"five milligrams" → "5 mg"`)

- Abbreviations spelled out unless registered

- Dates normalized to `MMM D, YYYY`

- Vital signs normalized (`120 over 80` → `120/80`)

- Medical units normalized (`mg`, `mL`, `mmHg`)

Configure formatting per deployment in the Console.
