---
title: "Recording Audio Best Practices"
description: "Best practices for audio capture"
---

- Capture at 16 kHz 16-bit PCM mono

- Place the mic 15–20 cm from the speaker

- Avoid hard reflective surfaces; soft furnishings improve quality

- Disable aggressive AGC; let SNR speak for itself

- Run a 5-second warmup before the encounter starts

- Monitor `audio.event` for live quality feedback
