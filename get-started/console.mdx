The [Medera Console](https://app.medera.ai) is the self-service portal where you create an account, manage organizations, generate client credentials, and monitor usage.

## Sign up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an account">
    Visit [app.medera.ai/signup](https://app.medera.ai/signup) and create an account with your work email. New tenants start in the **sandbox** environment with synthetic PHI only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email">
    Click the verification link we send. Without verification, you can still browse the Console but cannot create credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create an organization">
    Every tenant in Medera is an Organization. The Organization Provisioning Service walks you through a 16-step setup — branding, EHR integration, payer setup, screener defaults, and crisis escalation policy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate client credentials">
    Under **Settings → Developer API Keys**, generate a `client_id` / `client_secret` pair. Keys are scoped to a project and an environment (sandbox or production).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Environments

Medera supports three environments:

| Environment | Purpose | PHI policy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **sandbox** | Build and test against synthetic data | No real PHI permitted |
| **staging** | Pre-production verification under your BAA | Real PHI permitted under BAA |
| **production** | Live patient traffic | Real PHI under BAA + RLS + full audit |

Promote a tenant from sandbox to production by signing a Business Associate Agreement and completing the Trust Center checklist.
