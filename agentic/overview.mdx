The Medera Agentic Framework lets product and clinical teams compose **task-specific agents** from a typed registry of clinical experts. It ships with **24 production agents** and a canvas for building your own.

## Three layers

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Orchestrator" icon="layout-grid" href="/agentic/orchestrator">
    The top-level coordination layer that routes messages, manages context, and enforces policy.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Agents" icon="layers" href="/agentic/quickstart">
    24 production agents from Patient Intake to Evidence Synthesis. Each agent bundles experts and exposes a typed interface.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Experts" icon="puzzle" href="/agentic/experts/overview">
    The typed expert registry. Examples: PHQ-9 Screener Expert, C-SSRS Risk Expert, ICD-10 Coding Expert, PubMed Expert.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## The 24 agents

| Category | Agents |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Clinical Operations** | Patient Intake, Triage, Visit Scribe, Co-Therapy, Prior Authorization, Care Coordinator |
| **Documentation & Coding** | Medical Coding, CDI, Note Completeness, Referral Generator, Discharge Summary |
| **Patient-Facing** | Health Coach, Patient Education, Crisis Response, Outcomes Measurement |
| **Revenue Cycle & Ops** | Denial Appeals, Eligibility Verification, Claims Review, Quality Reporting |
| **Evidence & Safety** | Clinical Guidelines, Medication Reconciliation, Drug Interaction, Risk Stratification, Evidence Synthesis |

## Build your own

The [Agent Builder Canvas](/agentic/canvas/overview) lets product teams compose agents from a registry of 16 node types — start, end, agents, tools, logic, transforms.
