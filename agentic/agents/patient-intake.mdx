## Overview

The Patient Intake Agent runs the first contact a patient has with the practice. It is voice-first, runs in the patient's preferred language, and adapts question-by-question based on what the patient says and how they say it.

Every intake produces a clinician-ready packet: identity, consent, insurance, validated screeners, ICD-10 candidates from the chief complaint, and a multimodal acuity read from vocal prosody and linguistic content.

## How this agent works

### Configuration

This agent takes the following configuration from your practice settings:

- Practice intake template (fields, screeners, scripts)
- Routing rules by acuity and payer
- Identity verification and insurance capture settings
- Preferred language(s) and reading-level constraints
- Crisis escalation policy and handoff destinations

### Execution

At runtime, the agent executes the following steps:

1. Greets the patient and confirms identity, insurance, and consent
2. Conducts an adaptive interview against the practice intake template
3. Administers validated screeners (PHQ-9, GAD-7, AUDIT-C, C-SSRS) at appropriate decision points
4. Listens to vocal prosody and linguistic content for risk and acuity signals
5. Produces a structured session summary, ICD-10 candidates, and a clinician handoff packet

## Composable experts

The Patient Intake Agent bundles the following experts from the [registry](/agentic/experts/overview):

| Expert | Role |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Vocal Prosody Expert** | Pitch, rate, jitter, shimmer for affect and acuity inference |
| **Linguistic Content Expert** | Topic, valence, certainty, and pronoun-shift markers |
| **Validated Screener Expert** | PHQ-9, GAD-7, AUDIT-C, C-SSRS administration and scoring |
| **C-SSRS Risk Expert** | Suicide-risk classification with documented rationale |

## Typical use cases

- Front-load 30-minute intakes into a 6-minute adaptive interview the patient drives
- Capture validated screener data directly into the chart, not into a paper packet
- Surface acuity and risk signal to the clinician before the first session begins
- Translate intake content into the patient's preferred language without losing fidelity

## Compliance & safety guarantees

- Adaptive interview never replaces a validated screener — both run, both are documented
- Crisis-language is detected by C-SSRS criteria and routed to a human within seconds
- Every claim in the summary is anchored to a quoted span in the transcript
