## Overview

The Co-Therapy Agent is a silent clinical partner — it never speaks to the patient. It listens, watches, and reads, fuses the signal into 15 RDoC construct activations, and surfaces what matters to the clinician on a private channel.

Every construct activation is anchored to vocal, facial, and linguistic feature contributions so the clinician can verify and document with rationale.

## How this agent works

### Configuration

This agent takes the following configuration from your practice settings:

- Multimodal channels (audio, video, language)
- RDoC construct set the clinician cares about
- Practice notes template and live-feedback policy
- Patient consent and recording policy

### Execution

At runtime, the agent executes the following steps:

1. Captures multimodal signal — vocal prosody, facial affect, linguistic content
2. Computes RDoC construct activation in 15 documented constructs
3. Surfaces in-session signal to the clinician — never to the patient
4. Drafts the session note with construct-anchored claims
5. Captures longitudinal measurement data for outcomes review

## Composable experts

The Co-Therapy Agent bundles the following experts from the [registry](/agentic/experts/overview):

| Expert | Role |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Vocal Prosody Expert** | Pitch, rate, jitter, shimmer, energy |
| **Affect Dynamics Expert** | Facial affect intensity, valence, congruence |
| **Neurobehavioral Construct Expert** | Activation, valence, engagement, cognitive load |
| **Linguistic Content Expert** | Topic, valence, pronoun shift, certainty |

## Typical use cases

- Surface affect congruence and engagement to the clinician in real time
- Track 15 RDoC constructs longitudinally without an extra screener
- Document multimodal signal in the session note for defensibility
- Catch dissociation and crisis markers that the clinician misses on a tired Friday

## Compliance & safety guarantees

- Co-Therapy never addresses or interacts with the patient
- Every construct activation reports feature contributions and confidence
- Recording policy is explicit per patient and per session
