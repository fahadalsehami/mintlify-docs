Computes vocal prosody features from audio for downstream agent reasoning.

**Inputs:** audio stream or recording (≥ 10 s, 16 kHz)
**Outputs:** F0, jitter, shimmer, HNR, speaking rate, prosodic flatness, confidence

See [Vocal Acoustic Engine](/multimodal/vocal/overview).
