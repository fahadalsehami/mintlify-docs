---
title: "Co-Therapy Web Component"
description: "Drop-in multimodal capture UI"
--- The Co-Therapy Web Component captures audio + video and surfaces neurobehavioral construct activations to a clinician-only channel. ```html
<medera-cotherapy access-token="..." session-id="..." rdoc-constructs='["acute_threat_fear", "reward_responsiveness", "arousal"]'
></medera-cotherapy>
``` The component never shows signal to the patient — it surfaces a private clinician channel only.
