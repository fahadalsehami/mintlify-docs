The Medera public roadmap shows what's shipping next quarter. For confidential roadmap items under NDA, contact your account team.

## Q3 2026

- **Vocal Acoustic Engine v2** — extended prosodic features with sub-second windowing
- **RDoC v2** — refinements to the 15 documented constructs with updated feature contributions
- **CDI Agent** — outpatient specialty rule packs (BH, primary care, dermatology)
- **EHR write adapter** — write-back to NextGen and eClinicalWorks
- **Spanish (US) Intake** — validated translation pack for PHQ-9, GAD-7, AUDIT-C

## Q4 2026

- **HITRUST certification** — final report
- **Co-Therapy v2** — group session multimodal fusion
- **PA Appeals v2** — auto-generated peer-to-peer talking points
- **FHIR R5 read/write adapter**

## Future

- **Asynchronous video pipelines** — frame-rate-adaptive multimodal analysis on uploaded video
- **Voice cloning prevention** — speaker verification on every voice agent
- **EU data residency** — full EU stack with EU-only sub-processors
